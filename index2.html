🗣️ Nihali Language Isolate

💥 CRITICALLY ENDANGERED (~2,500 Native Speakers Remaining)

Spoken primarily in the isolated Satpura forest hills along the borderlands of Madhya Pradesh and Maharashtra, India. Nihali is a pure geographic language isolate, possessing absolute zero structural or historical links to Indo-Aryan, Dravidian, or neighboring Austroasiatic language groups.

Crisis Level: Real-Time Collapse. As native elders pass away, this completely independent branch of human cognitive development faces full deletion from evolutionary history within decades.

🏔️ Burushaski Language Isolate

⚠️ VULNERABLE (~100,000 Valley Speakers Remaining)

Preserved within the high-altitude valleys of Hunza, Nagar, and Yasin in northern Pakistan. Like Nihali, Burushaski stands completely isolated against all neighboring global language trees. It serves as an ancient living structural fossil representing pre-historic migrations across South Asia.

Platform Strategy: System uses sparse localized dictionaries to synthetically map sentence templates, protecting advanced cultural vocabulary variants from extinction.
